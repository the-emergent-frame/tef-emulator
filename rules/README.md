# Candidate rules

Implemented scaffold: [source-channels-v0.1](source-channels-v0.1/README.md). Its independent channels, phase increments, and dimensionless transport weight are candidate assumptions, with no physical energy transfer law assigned.

Each proposed rule set should record:

- Identifier, version, and research question.
- Inherited paper assumptions and references to their precise versions.
- New assumptions and their motivation.
- Input state, applicability conditions, and output state.
- Dependency, conflict, and event-selection semantics.
- Units, transfer accounting, and intended invariants.
- Whether probabilities, phases, or amplitudes are modeled, and how they compose.
- Observables, controls, and conditions that would reject the proposal.

Version a rule when its physical assumptions change. Keep failed or superseded proposals identifiable so experiments remain interpretable.
